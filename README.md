# Airbnb Business Intelligence Analysis

Power BI dashboard analyzing Airbnb's global listings and reviews marketplace, built as a simulated CEO project brief.

## Business Problem

Give leadership an executive view of the listings marketplace — size, pricing, performance, and customer experience — to support data-driven decisions.

## Dataset

Airbnb Listings & Reviews dataset covering 10 major cities.

**Source:** [Maven Analytics — Airbnb Listings & Reviews](https://mavenanalytics.io/data-playground/airbnb-listings-reviews)

## Tools

**Power BI · Power Query · DAX**

## Key Insights

1. **Paris has the largest listing share** at 23.1%, while Hong Kong represents 2.5% of total listings.

2. **30.8% of listings have zero reviews**, despite average ratings across cities generally remaining around 93–95. This unreviewed segment warrants further investigation into new versus potentially inactive listings.

3. **Superhosts have higher average ratings** than Regular Hosts (97 vs. 92), while Superhosts represent approximately 24K of 182K unique hosts.

4. **Cleanliness is the weakest review dimension**, with an average score of 9.31 compared with 9.70 for location and communication.

*Note: Prices are not currency-normalized across cities, so cross-city price comparisons should be interpreted directionally. Review-based metrics cover approximately 69% of listings.*
